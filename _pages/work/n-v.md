**N / V** is an audiovisual duo with the visual artist
[Victor Calvi](https://victorcalvi.com/). The two media are not layered — sound
and generative image are built as one environment and played together, in real
time, on custom tools.

The subject of the set is the tension between control and its loss. Both systems
are driven hard enough that structure destabilises, and the material worth
keeping is whatever emerges at that edge. I play the sound — hardware synthesis,
processing, and generative models handled as instruments rather than as
generators. Victor drives the visual system, which moves at the same tempo.

## Performances

The duo has presented the work at **Alte Münze** in Berlin for the *Blank Page*
series, and at **STUDIO1111** for the opening of Berlin New Media Week.
