I apply deep learning to music to create tools and real-time systems across three core domains.

**Industry.** Audio Research Engineer at [Native Instruments](https://www.native-instruments.com/en/), applying deep learning to next-generation sound technology and developing product features.

**Artistic practice.** Producer, performer and DJ under the name **Ninon** — electronic music influenced by cinematic deep techno and modular synthesis.

**Research.** PhD, [IRCAM](https://www.ircam.fr) / Sorbonne Université, [ACIDS](https://acids-ircam.github.io/) group — [*Creative Deep Learning on Real Time Embedded Architectures*](/research/phd/), defended in May 2025. The work covers neural audio synthesis and model-driven instruments, including the [Neurorack](/projects/neurorack/).

I also teach [Ableton](/teaching/ableton/) and [Pure Data](/teaching/puredata/) on the [ATIAM](https://atiam.ircam.fr) master at IRCAM / Sorbonne Université; student work is on [SoundCloud](https://soundcloud.com/atiam-ircam/sets).

Always open to collaborations — artistic, academic, or hybrid.
